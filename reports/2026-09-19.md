It uses the model's next-token probability mass to score declared alternatives directly, eliminating extra decoding and validation.
2. **Key innovations**: Choosekit introduces a novel approach to typed probabilistic decisions, leveraging the capabilities of the llama.cpp model interface to provide a seamless interface between the model and the application state.
